🎓 Apprenticeships

Off-the-job training recorded at the moment it happens, rather than reconstructed at the end of the year. In childcare most of the teaching is a room leader showing an apprentice something on the floor — and practical training, shadowing and mentoring all count, provided the learning is new and it is written down.

The 20% rule ended on 1 August 2025. Each standard now publishes its own minimum, and Wren stores that figure with the page it came from rather than working it out as a percentage of anybody's hours. Recognition of prior learning may reduce it, but never below 187 hours.

      Staff under 18

      A worker under 18 has tighter limits than an adult: 8 hours a day and 40 a week, a 30-minute break once the day is over four and a half hours, 12 hours' rest between days and 48 hours in the week. The rota warns when a shift breaks one — it never refuses to save it. Anyone with no date of birth on file cannot be checked.
