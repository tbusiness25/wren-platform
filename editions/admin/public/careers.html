What it's like to work here

Small, settled, and not a chain

We're two buildings on a quiet residential street in Ealing — a Baby Room for up to ten children aged 6 months to 2 years, and a Pre-school Room for 2 to 5 year olds. That's the whole nursery. You will know every child's name, and every child will know yours.

Because we're small, nobody here is anonymous. If you have an idea for the sensory conservatory, you say it on Monday and it can be happening by Wednesday. There's no regional manager to run it past. The flip side is honest to say out loud: on a short-staffed day everyone mucks in, including whoever is meant to be in the office.

  • People stayMany of our team have been here 15–25 years. If you join us, you're joining people who already know each other well — and who will teach you a great deal.
  • Proper meals, cooked hereOur cook the chef makes breakfast, a two-course lunch and tea fresh on site every day. Staff eat with the children. We're a nut-free nursery.
  • Real development, not a tick-boxSupervisions actually happen, CPD is paid for, and if you're working towards a Level 3 we'll build the hours around your study.
  • Hours you can plan a life aroundWe're open Monday to Friday, 8am–6pm. We close on bank holidays and we don't do weekends.
  • Outdoors and messy, in all weathersSand and water, loose parts, murals, a garden, and an all-weather conservatory that doubles as a sensory room and a yoga space.

Our history

A family nursery since 1990

Your Nursery has been part of our family for three generations. My mum ran it, and my earliest memories are of being brought here on school holidays and sick days. My brothers and I earned our pocket money at weekends fixing fences, cleaning carpets and tending the garden.

I did my work experience here, spent my gap year as the nursery chef and covered shifts in the under-2s room — which is where I found out that childcare was the thing I actually wanted to do. I came back after university, completed my Level 3, worked in the baby room for ten years, and went on to gain Early Years Teacher Status. When my mum retired in 2019, I took over as manager.

The nursery has grown up alongside the families who use it. We're proud members of the Ealing Early Years Quality Partnership, and we work closely with parents so that nothing about their child's day is a mystery to them.

Written by the owner — do ask about any of it when we meet.

Our ethos

What we expect of ourselves, and of you

Children learn best when they feel safe, known and unhurried. Everything below follows from that.

The child sets the paceWe follow interests rather than marching everyone through the same activity. A child who wants forty minutes at the water tray gets forty minutes.
Every child has their personThe key person relationship is the backbone of the day — for the child, and for the parent handing them over at the door.
Play is the workWe plan for play and we defend it. Adults join in at the child's level rather than directing from above.
Safeguarding is everybody's jobNot just the DSL's. If something niggles, you say it the same day. We would far rather hear a worry that turns out to be nothing.
Parents are partnersObservations, diaries and messages go home the same day. No surprises at parents' evening.
We say what we meanWith children, with families, and with each other. Kindly, and without leaving things unsaid.
