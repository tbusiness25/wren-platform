Billing changes
Everything that moves this child's invoice, in one place. Change it here, and Wren tells you straight away which invoices no longer match — and offers the right fix for each one. Nothing is ever emailed from this panel.
1. Days & room
A change of days or room starts a new booking pattern from the date you give. The pattern is what the fee is priced from, so this is the change that moves a bill most.
Times, one-offs and history →
2. Funded hours
Whether this child gets funding, and how many hours a week, for a term. The effective dates are the term's own — pick the term the change belongs to.
Declaration, codes and the full record →
3. Discounts
Attaching a discount is a decision, never automatic. It applies from the date you give.
The full discount panel →
4. A one-off price for a month
Overrides the catalogue day rate for whole billing months, without touching the funding. The invoice line says "price changed". Used by Generate, Update, the Income Forecast and Reissue alike — one figure, everywhere.
5. An extra session
A one-off day on top of the pattern, at this child's normal session rate. It goes onto the family's next invoice automatically, and is billed exactly once.

Book a session
The booking pattern is where this child's room and days actually live. It drives the morning register, the sign-in buttons, occupancy, the ratios, the kitchen headcount and the fees — so a child with no pattern is counted by none of them.
