How to find a fob UID Log into your door access controller (its admin web UI on your LAN) → Access → Card Management → scan the fob — the UID (e.g. A1B2C3D4) appears in the card number field. Alternatively, tap them on the door reader/intercom and check the unknown-fob alerts — the UID will be in the alert message.
Why this list exists Staff leave through the emergency exit without tapping out. Wren does not guess a leaving time — the gap becomes a question the person answers the next morning, and their answer is recorded as self-reported so a timesheet always says how each time was captured. The rota time is offered on their screen as a starting point and is never written unless they choose it.

Direction comes from the reader The gate reader is IN — staff have to tap it to get through the gate, so a tap there can only mean arriving. The office reader is OUT. A reader set to both falls back to alternating taps, which is what a one-reader setting has to do and is exactly what caused people to be clocked out while stood in the building.

The webhook that receives taps is public and unauthenticated (the door intercom cannot send a login). So a tap is only recorded if it matches an active reader below — by device id, by a door-name alias, or by source IP. Everything else is refused.
