📕 Staff Handbook & Consultation

Eighteen chapters on the EY portal, each under five minutes, each ending in one closed question about a concrete alternative. That shape is the whole point: the framework consultation was an open invitation to comment and got zero replies, so this one never asks "what do you think of…" — it asks "which of these two would you rather use", which has an obvious first tap.

It is attributed, and the page says so. Every answer carries a name so you can ask the people who have not answered, and so you can go back to somebody about what they wrote. Nudges are in-app only and capped; anybody who has submitted is never nudged again, and nothing about this is ever emailed to anyone.
