What your child will be learning this year

Each room plans around a theme for every half-term — the books we will read together, the songs we will sing, what we hope the children will get from it, and the activities we have planned. This is the same plan the room team works from, published here so you can see it and talk about it at home.

Plans are a starting point, not a timetable. We follow the children's interests, so what actually happens in the room will always include things that are not on this page.
