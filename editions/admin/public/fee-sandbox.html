Fee Modeller

A sandbox for next year's fee structure — change the rates, see every family's bill move.
Nothing here changes a live fee. This screen writes only to its own scenario tables; applying a structure to the real fee schedule is a separate, deliberate step. Every child on the roll is priced through the same engine that writes your invoices — their booked days, their room, their band (age on the 1st of the month, Pre-school never below the 2-year rate), this term's funding, consumables and standing discounts. Now is the live fee schedule, checked against each child's current invoice. New is the scenario, with nothing changed but the rates. A month is 52 weeks ÷ 12, a day is 10 hours, funded hours are £0.
