Fee Schedule & Itemised Billing
Statutory itemised billing (DfE, Jan 2026). Funded hours are always free and appear on every invoice as £0 lines showing the hours delivered. Meals, consumables and activities are voluntary — each is individually opt-out-able and opting out never affects the funded place. Lines are grouped into the five statutory categories. The “per funded hour equivalent” shown anywhere is display-only and never stored.
