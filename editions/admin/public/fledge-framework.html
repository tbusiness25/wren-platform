🪶 Fledge — our progress framework
Wren's open Early Years curriculum framework — the curriculum this setting tracks against
⬇ Download PDF
What this is. Fledge is our progress framework: it describes what a child can typically do by the end of a band, so you can notice whether a child is where you would expect. It is not our curriculum — the curriculum is our termly planning (the theme, the books, the songs and the activities), which says what we teach, in what order, and how. Underneath, it is Wren's own non-statutory Early Years framework — in the same category as Development Matters and Birth to 5 Matters, and designed to be owned and edited by each setting. It uses five best-fit developmental bands (B1 Nestlings → B5 Ready to fledge) across the seven EYFS areas. In the tracker, Fledge appears as the Fledge bands cohort view — a best-fit lens, not a tick-list. This is the full reference document.
