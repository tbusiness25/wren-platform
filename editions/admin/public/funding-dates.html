💷 Funding dates

Used for funding: entitlement hours, rates and what the council pays.
Three funding periods a year — Autumn 1 Sep – 31 Dec, Spring 1 Jan – 31 Mar, Summer 1 Apr – 31 Aug. Funding runs continuously across holidays; the rates the council pays per band live on the same period.
These are not the teaching half-terms. Planning, weekly plans and the breaks between them live on Settings → Term dates →. The two calendars are separate on purpose: funding runs continuously, teaching does not.
