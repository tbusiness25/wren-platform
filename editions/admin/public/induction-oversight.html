Onboard a new starter

Put their email in and Wren creates their (inactive) staff record, assigns the induction workbook, and emails them a welcome with a link to read week 1 before day one.
What the email says. A warm welcome, what Wren is, and their own induction link — plus, because this is a safeguarding-relevant account, that Wren holds children's personal data and that their access is logged. No PIN is ever emailed. You set their PIN with them in person on their first day, and their account stays inactive until you approve it.
