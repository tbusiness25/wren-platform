Key-Person Planning

Pick a practitioner and Wren will draft a termly update for each of their key children, from everything on record for that child — every observation, every next step, every previous update — with activities for here and ideas for home.

Each draft goes to that practitioner's review screen and to their notifications. Nothing is sent to a family by this page. Drafting runs on the setting's own hardware and children's names are removed before the model is asked anything.
