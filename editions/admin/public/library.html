📖 Reading Room

Every policy, the handbook, courses, uploaded paperwork and more — one catalogue, browsable by area. Staff and parents each see their own slice; this is the full view. New uploads land in Needs review until an area and audience are set. Open anything and it tells you where it came from and whether what you are reading is the whole item; once you are done with something, Archive takes it off the shelf and Delete takes it out of the room for good.
