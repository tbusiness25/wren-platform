🧭 Start here

The four apps, and how to find your way around Roost.

I'm still logging in as Toby — how do I sign in as myself?

You already have your own account. Signing in as yourself (not Toby) means the system knows it's really you — which matters for safeguarding records, messages you send, and anything you approve.

  1. Go to admin.example-nursery.co.uk in a browser (save it as a bookmark or an icon on your home screen).
  2. Enter your own work email and tap Continue →.
  3. Enter your own PIN on the number pad that appears. (Toby sets this for you — see "How do I reset a PIN" below if you don't have one yet.)
  4. Tick "Trust this device for 30 days" if this is your own phone or computer — then you won't have to enter your PIN every time.

If it doesn't work: "Incorrect PIN" means exactly that — ask Toby to reset it for you (see the Logins topic below). Don't keep guessing; a few wrong tries will temporarily lock the login.

What are the four parts of Wren, and who uses which?

Wren is one system split into four addresses, each built for a different job.

  1. admin.example-nursery.co.uk — "Roost", the office system. Managers only. Everything in this guide.
  2. ey.example-nursery.co.uk — the room tablets. Every practitioner uses this for the register, diary, observations.
  3. hr.example-nursery.co.uk — the staff app, for everyone's own phone. Rota, holiday requests, payslips, CPD.
  4. parents.example-nursery.co.uk — the parents app. Parents only.

All four talk to the same records underneath — a child's details only need entering once.

How do I find something quickly in Roost?

Roost has a search box that jumps straight to any page, child or member of staff.

  1. Press Ctrl + K on the keyboard anywhere in Roost — or tap the Search button at the top.
  2. Start typing a name or the thing you're after.
  3. Press Enter, or tap the result.
What's the sidebar, and what's "What needs me"?

The left-hand menu groups everything into ten areas (Home, Children, Staff, Admissions, Finance, Curriculum, Comms, Operations, AI & Intelligence, Help & Training). The 🏠 Home row always takes you to Cockpit — your daily landing page.

  1. Tap 🏠 Home in the sidebar to get back to Cockpit from anywhere.
  2. Tap 🎯 What needs me (also under Home) for a single list of everything waiting on a manager — nothing is hidden on a separate page.

🔑 Logins

Staff, tablets, and parents — how everyone gets in, and what to do when they can't.

How do staff sign in to Roost or the staff app?

Email, then a PIN — the same login on Roost and on the staff app (hr.example-nursery.co.uk). Anyone who has set up a Wren ID can use that instead (see the Wren ID question below).

  1. Enter your work email, tap Continue →.
  2. Enter your PIN on the number pad.
  3. Tick "Trust this device for 30 days (don't use on shared devices)" on your own phone or laptop — never on a shared room tablet.

A few staff also have an extra six-digit code step (two-factor) — if you see that, it's because it was switched on for your account specifically.

How does a room tablet get set up?

A tablet has to be "enrolled" once before it can show any child's details — this stops a lost or stolen tablet from being any use to whoever finds it.

  1. On the tablet, sign in as any member of staff first.
  2. Go to More → Settings → IT Settings.
  3. Tap + Enrol this device.

To see every enrolled tablet, or remove one that's lost or being replaced:

  1. In Roost, go to Settings → Devices & Security.
  2. Find the tablet in the list and tap Revoke to remove it.

If it doesn't work: a brand-new, unenrolled tablet will refuse to show any child's record — that's it working correctly, not a fault. Enrol it first.

How do I reset a member of staff's PIN?

Two ways — pick whichever is quicker.

  1. From their profile: Roost → Staff → open their name → tap Reset PIN (it says Set PIN if they've never had one).
  2. From the full list: Roost → Staff → Staff PINs — shows everyone and when their PIN was last set, so you can reset or remove one without opening their profile first.

If it doesn't work: Toby's own PIN can't be reset this way on purpose — only Toby can change it himself.

What is Wren ID, and how do I send someone a new link when theirs has expired?

Wren ID is a second way into Roost and the staff app: an email address and a password the person chooses themselves. Their PIN keeps working too. Each person gets an email with a set-up link; the link lasts 14 days, and it stops working once it has been opened, so someone who opened it and didn't finish needs a new one.

  1. Roost → sidebar Staff group → 🔑 Permissions.
  2. Open the Wren ID tab.
  3. Find the person. Their row says where they are: Password set (done), Invited, Opened (started but didn't finish) or Link expired.
  4. Tap Re-send on their row. A fresh link goes to their email and the old one stops working.
  5. To send to several people at once, tick them and tap Send invitation at the top.

If it doesn't work: if their row says No Wren ID yet, tap Create their Wren ID first, then Re-send. If the link never arrives, check the email on their staff record and their spam folder. You need to be signed in as yourself, not as Toby, to see who sent what.

How do I get a new starter their login?

A new starter fills in their own details through a one-off link you send them — you don't have to set up their account by hand.

  1. Roost → Staff → Induction.
  2. Tap ✉️ Onboard new starter by email.
  3. Fill in their name and email, tap Create & send welcome.
  4. They get an email with a link to fill in their own details — no login needed for that part.
  5. Once they've submitted it, come back to Induction and tap Approve & activate next to their name — this is what actually creates their login.
How does a parent sign in for the first time?

A parent never has a password to remember — they get a one-time code by email every time they sign in.

  1. They go to parents.example-nursery.co.uk.
  2. They enter the email address you have on file for them.
  3. A one-time code lands in their inbox; they type it in to get access.

This part of the login screen is currently run by our security provider (Cloudflare), not by Wren itself — so the page will briefly look slightly different from the rest of the parents app. That's expected.

How do I add or change a parent's email address?

A parent's email is also their login — get it right, or they can't sign in.

  1. Roost → Children → open the child → the 👪 Family tab.
  2. Edit the Email field under Parent/Carer 1 or 2, then save.

Want to double-check what a parent actually sees? On the same Family tab there's a 👁️ View [Name]'s Finch portal button — it shows you their app exactly as they see it, for 30 minutes, and it's all logged.

A parent says the code didn't arrive — what do I do?

There's no "resend" button inside Wren for this today — the code comes from our security provider's own page, not from Wren.

  1. Check the email address on file is spelled correctly (Family tab, above).
  2. Ask them to check their spam / junk folder.
  3. Ask them to try again after a minute — codes do occasionally arrive late.
  4. If it still doesn't arrive, ask Toby — this is one to escalate rather than something you can fix from Roost.
A parent says they see no child in the app — what's happened?

Usually this means the email they signed in with isn't the one linked to their child's record.

  1. Check the Family tab (above) for the exact email address on file.
  2. Make sure it matches, character for character, what the parent is typing in.
  3. If it genuinely doesn't match anything, ask Toby — a handful of newer sign-in routes show the parent a clear "ask the nursery to set one up" message, but the main one does not explain itself, by design.
Someone is locked out — what can I actually do?

There's no self-service "I forgot my PIN" button for staff — a manager has to step in.

  1. A member of staff: reset their PIN yourself (see above).
  2. A parent: check their email on file, and see the two questions above.
  3. Yourself, if you're the one locked out: ask Toby — only he can reset his own login, and only another manager can reset yours.

If it doesn't work: too many wrong PIN attempts locks the login for a short time automatically — wait a few minutes before trying again rather than repeating the same PIN.

🌱 Enquiries, waiting list, offers and starting a new child

From a first enquiry through to a child's first day.

Where do I see new enquiries and the waiting list?

One area covers the whole journey from enquiry to registered child.

  1. Roost sidebar → Admissions.
  2. Use the tabs across the top to move between occupancy, the pipeline (every enquiry's current stage) and the offer engine.
How do I take a family from enquiry to starting with us?

Every step happens from the family's card in Admissions. The card moves along the pipeline by itself as you go, and never moves backwards. The button for the next step is always the highlighted one. Nothing is emailed to the family until you've seen the email and pressed Send.

  1. Viewing: book it from the card. When the room confirms it, you see the confirmation email and choose Send or Don't send.
  2. Waiting list: on the card, tap → Waiting list.
  3. Offer: on the Waiting list tab, tap Offer. Check the pre-filled email (fill in any [CHECK: …] line, e.g. the deposit), then Send offer, or Mark as offered without emailing if you've told them another way.
  4. Their answer: tap Accepted or Declined on their row.
  5. Registration: once accepted, tap ✉️ Send registration link…. Check the email and press Send, or Copy link instead to send it by WhatsApp.
  6. Approve: when they've filled it in, they appear in Children → pending. Approve them there. Their parents-app access, billing email and payment reference are set up automatically.

Deposit: when it arrives in the bank, use Finance → Reconcile → Deposit… on that line and type their surname. It finds them at any stage. We don't hold deposits: it goes onto their account and comes off their first bill.

Where do I record a deposit or set up settling-in sessions?

Once a child has accepted a place, their deposit and settling-in plan live on their own profile, not a separate admissions page.

  1. Roost → Children → open the child's profile.
  2. Open the 🌱 Onboarding tab.
  3. Tap Raise deposit (2 weeks fees) to note what's due. When it's paid, tap Pay: it goes onto their account as a payment and comes off their first bill (we don't hold deposits). If the money came in by bank transfer, record it from Reconcile → Deposit… instead.
  4. Below it, edit the settling-in schedule — each session's length can be changed (15 minutes to 10 hours).

👶 Children

One profile per child holds everything — find it, and everything else follows.

How do I find a child and see their full record?

Every child has one profile page with a row of tabs across the top — details, family, observations, diary, funding and more all live in the same place.

  1. Roost sidebar → Children, or press Ctrl+K and type their name.
  2. Tap the child to open their profile.
  3. Use the tabs along the top — Details, Family, All About Me, Observations, Daily Diary, Funding & Invoices and the rest — to move between the different parts of their record.
Where do I update allergies or medical details?

Allergy and medical information lives on the child's profile, under the details about who they are.

  1. Open the child's profile → the All About Me tab.
  2. Find Food Allergies (and the other medical fields nearby) and edit them.
  3. Save.

If it doesn't work: there are currently two separate places allergy information can be entered on a child's record — if something looks like it hasn't "taken", check whether it needs updating in both. [Toby to confirm which two, and whether this has been tidied up]

Where do I assign or change a child's key person?

Key person planning has its own tool, separate from the child's main profile.

  1. Roost sidebar → Curriculum → Key-Person Planning.

How do room moves work?

Wren moves a child up a room automatically on the Monday on or after their 2nd birthday, unless you've told it otherwise.

  1. Open the child's profile and find the Room moves section.
  2. If a move needs to happen on a different date, set that there — once you have, the automatic rule leaves that child alone.
A child is leaving — how do I send a keepsake and update their record?

Keepsakes is where a leaving child's memory book is put together and sent — it's the only place this happens.

  1. Roost → Comms → Keepsakes.
  2. Find the child and tap 🎁 Generate keepsake & link.
  3. To write the farewell message that goes inside it, follow the link on that page through to Leavers Book — write the message, tap ✨ Regenerate AI message if you'd like a starting draft, then 💾 Save and 📄 Finalise & Download PDF.

If it doesn't work: Leavers Book isn't in the sidebar or the Ctrl+K search — you can only reach it via the link inside Keepsakes, so start there.

📋 Daily running

The register, ratios, the diary, sleep checks and the kitchen — mostly room-tablet jobs, with a manager's view on top.

Where is the register, and how do I check ratios?

The register itself is signed on the room tablets; Roost gives you the overview and the compliance record.

  1. Day-to-day signing in/out happens on the room tablet: More → Child Register.
  2. For the manager's ratio view: Roost → Operations → Room Headcounts — shows each scheduled check for the day per room (done, missed, or under ratio), with a Today and History tab.
Where do staff write the daily diary, and can I see it from Roost?

The diary is written on the room tablet as it happens; you can see any child's diary from their profile.

  1. Staff write it on the tablet from the room's home screen.
  2. To check a child's diary from Roost: open their profile → Daily Diary tab.
Where are the menus and kitchen records?

Menus are set up in Roost; the kitchen's day-to-day logs are their own page.

  1. Roost → Operations → Kitchen for the daily SFBB food-safety logs.
  2. Menu planning itself: Settings → Food & Menus.

🎓 Learning

Observations, next steps, tracking against the framework, and planning.

Where do I see a child's observations?

Observations are written on the tablet by the key person; you can read them from Roost at any time.

  1. Open the child's profile → the Observations tab.
How do "next steps" work now?

A next step is something specific a child's key person is working on with them right now — kept short on purpose, rather than a long list nobody reviews.

  1. A key person sets one from an observation, from the child's page, or from a termly update — up to about three "active" at a time per child.
  2. They close one with a single tap on the tablet once the child can do it, ideally linked to the observation that shows it.
  3. At each half-term review, every active next step is marked achieved, carried forward or retired, and new ones are set.

On 5 October 2026 a one-off, Toby-approved tidy-up retired the old backlog of next steps imported from EyLog (most untouched for well over a year) so every child starts the new system with a small, current set.

Where do I see a child's progress against the framework?

The Tracker Grid shows every child against the whole framework at a glance.

  1. Roost sidebar → Curriculum → Tracker Grid.
Where are two-year checks and parent reports?

Both are written and reviewed from a child's own profile.

  1. Open the child's profile → the 2-Year Check tab, or the Reports tab for termly/transition reports.
Where is room and curriculum planning?

The termly and weekly plan that each room works from.

  1. Roost sidebar → Curriculum → Planning.

🛡️ Safeguarding, incidents, accidents, medicine

Who can see what, and where each kind of record lives.

Where do I review a safeguarding concern?

Every concern raised by staff lands in one manager queue for sign-off.

  1. Roost sidebar → Safeguarding.
  2. Use the tabs: concerns (active), sign-off queue, the full log, and the audit trail.
Where do accidents and incidents get signed off?

Staff log an accident or incident on the room tablet; a manager reviews and signs it off in Roost.

  1. Roost → Accidents & Incidents for anything about a specific child (reported from the room tablets).
  2. Roost → Setting Incidents for whole-building events — fire, intruder, a lost child, an outbreak — which is a separate list from child accidents.
Who can see medicine records?

Medicine records aren't automatically manager-only any more — each member of staff can be individually given or refused access.

  2. To change who can see it: give or remove the medicine permission on that person's staff record.

💷 Finance

How money moves through Wren since October — invoices, the bank feed, funding and family statements.

How do monthly invoices get sent, and how do I fix one?

Invoices are not automatic — a manager runs them each month with one button, with a preview first.

  1. Roost → Finance → Invoices & Payments → Invoices tab → tap Generate invoices for a month… and pick the month.
  2. Wren shows a Preview of what it's about to create — check it, then tap Generate to actually send them. Children who already have an invoice for the period are skipped automatically.
  3. To fix a mistake on an invoice already sent: find it and use Regenerate (only works before it's locked/sent), Credit note (the usual way to correct a sent invoice), or Write off if it will never be paid.
How do I add a payment a family made (cash, a transfer, Tax-Free Childcare)?

For money that isn't waiting in the bank list on Reconcile — cash, a cheque, or anything you've been told about.

  1. Roost → Finance → Invoices & Payments → Payments tab → Add payment…
  2. Type the child's name and pick them, then fill in the date, payment mode and amount. Add the reference if there is one.
  3. Under Apply to, leave Oldest unpaid first unless the family told you which invoice it's for (A chosen invoice). On account (credit) keeps it as credit.
  4. Tap Add payment. The family's statement updates straight away.

A deposit is added the same way with payment mode Deposit — we don't hold deposits, so it goes straight onto the family's balance.

Made a mistake? Tap the payment in the list and choose Undo. It's marked as reversed, never deleted, and the invoices it paid go back to owing.

How do I give a family a credit, or take money off an invoice?
  1. Invoices & Payments → Payments tab → Add credit note…
  2. Pick the child, the invoice it's for (or none), the amount and a short reason everyone will understand.
  3. Tap to save — it shows on the family's statement at once.

You can also open any invoice from the Invoices tab and use Credit note… or Write off… there.

How does bank reconciliation work?

Every morning Wren pulls in yesterday's bank transactions automatically; it only ever suggests a match — you confirm it.

  1. Roost → Finance → Invoices & Payments → Reconcile tab for the quick list, or Open full workbench for everything below.
  2. For each unmatched line, Wren usually suggests which invoice it belongs to. If it's right, tap ✓ Confirm.
  3. If a payment covers more than one invoice, tap ✂ Split….
  4. If it's a deposit rather than an invoice payment, tap 🏷 Deposit….
  5. If it doesn't belong on this screen at all, tap 🗑 Remove….
  6. Matched something by mistake? Find it under "Recently matched" and tap ↩ Undo.

The bank feed itself is our Barclays account, connected automatically every morning at 7:15am — nobody needs to download or upload a statement.

What are the "LA-" and "TFC" references I see on invoices?

Two different reference numbers, both printed on every invoice.

  1. LA-XXXX is the family's own payment reference — Wren generates it, and it's what helps a payment match itself automatically.
  2. The other reference (4 letters + 5 digits, e.g. AJON12345) is the child's own Tax-Free Childcare reference from HMRC, used when a parent pays through that scheme.

If a family doesn't have a reference yet: Finance page → "Family payment references" section → Give every family a reference.

Can parents pay by card?

Yes — card payments went live on 1 October 2026, reached from the parents app's Payments page.

  1. A parent pays from parents.example-nursery.co.uk → Pay.
Where is funding — terms, codes, declarations, headcount?

All of funding lives on one page, split into tabs.

  1. For a quick look: Invoices & Payments → Funding tab (this term's funded children and hours). For everything else: Finance (full) → Funding.
  2. Tabs: Terms, Child Allocation, Summary & Submission, Council Portal, Declarations.
Where do I read a family's statement — what they owe and what they've paid?

A running ledger for the whole family, exactly as the parent sees it in their own app.

  1. Invoices & Payments → Families tab → type the child's name. Or open any one of the family's children → the 💷 Funding & Invoices tab.
  2. If the family has more than one child with us, this same ledger shows all of them together, with a running Balance.
Is Xero still used?

Not for day-to-day payments any more — the Barclays bank feed above is the live source for reconciling what's been paid. Xero is still connected, but now mainly for pulling reports (profit & loss, balance sheet) into Wren for a comparison view — it doesn't write anything back to Xero.

💬 Talking to parents and staff

The inbox, messages, newsletters, and keeping the team in the loop.

How does the Inbox work, and who can use it?

The Inbox is where email to the nursery is read and answered, with Wren drafting a reply for you to check first.

  1. Roost sidebar → Comms → Inbox (it's the first thing you see in Comms as of 5 October 2026).
  2. Open an email. If Wren has drafted a reply, read it over.
  3. Edit it if you need to, then tap Send — nothing ever goes out on its own; a person always presses Send.

Only staff specifically given access can use the Inbox — currently Toby, the office manager and the admissions lead. Anyone else who tries sees a message explaining why, rather than a blank page.

How do I tell the whole staff team something?

A running noticeboard for the team, with control over who it's aimed at.

  1. Roost sidebar → Comms → Tell the staff.
  2. Write your notice, choose who it's for, and publish it.
How do I send a newsletter?

Newsletters go out to whichever group of parents you choose, not always everyone.

  1. Roost sidebar → Comms → Newsletter.
  2. Write it, then choose the audience before sending.
How do I post a "What's new in Wren" update?

This is the page that tells the team (or parents) what's changed in Wren itself — and this very guide will likely be announced through it.

  1. Roost sidebar → Help & Training → What's new in Wren.
  2. Tap Write an update, fill it in, choose who sees it, and publish.
