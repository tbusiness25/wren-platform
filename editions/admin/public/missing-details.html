Funding week nudge (in the parents' app, never email)
Before each claim window closes, families with a gap that matters get a short reminder in their app, and again every few days until it is filled. It is off until you switch it on here, after seeing exactly who it would message and what. It then runs as a dry run (it logs what it would send, sends nothing) until you switch dry run off as well.
Reminder campaigns (in the parents' app, never email)
A campaign reminds each family every couple of days, plus the final day, until their items are filled in. It starts only when you press Start, after you have seen exactly what will be sent. Each family stops being reminded the moment their items are complete.
Turn the ones that are ours into accountable tasks
Key person not assigned, All About Me not marked complete, and each staff member's own DBS, right to work, training and qualification gaps become tasks for the right person. They get the daily nudge and the escalation, and they close by themselves when the record is complete. Nothing is created until you press Create.
