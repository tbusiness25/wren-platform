Surplus staffed time is time when a room had more staff than the booked children required. It is usually deliberate and usually good — settling a new child, SEND support, planning, supervision, a student on top of the count. Recording which turns invisible time into something the setting can talk about, and lets a practitioner answer “I had no off-ratio time” with data.
