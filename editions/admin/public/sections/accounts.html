Which page do I use? What each finance page is built from, and which one to rely on.

Some numbers appear on more than one page, and there are three different forecasts. Rule of thumb: money that has happened → Invoices & Payments (the accountant data pack ties to it). Money that will happen → Income forecast on Invoices & Payments.

Source of truth rely on it · Same data another view of a source of truth · Actual real stored figures, cut a different way · Estimate built on assumed or fallback rates · Preview not reconciled or tested — check before relying on it

Invoices & Payments

  • Who owesSource of truth
    Who owes what, by family.
    Built from The family ledger: invoices, credit notes and payments. The same balance parents see in their app.
  • InvoicesSource of truth
    Every invoice and credit note. Rely on this one — the accountant pack’s Invoices sheet is these rows.
    Built from invoices, as stored. Finance (full) → Invoices reads the same rows through the same API. Neither list hides demo/test children; the pack does.
  • PaymentsSource of truth
    Every payment recorded. Rely on this one.
    Built from payments, as stored, every status.
  • ReconcileSource of truth
    Bank money in that is not matched yet.
    Built from The live bank feed: unmatched lines only. Finance (full) → Reconcile is the same queue with the deeper tools.
  • FundingEstimate
    This term’s funded hours and roughly what they are worth.
    Built from Each child’s funded hours this term × the term’s council rates.
  • FamiliesSource of truth
    One family’s whole account.
    Built from The same family ledger as Who owes.
  • Income forecastEstimate
    What the next invoice runs will bill. The forecast to rely on.
    Built from A dry run of the same invoice generator that raises the real invoices, month by month. Nothing is saved.
  • Accountant data packSource of truth
    The raw rows behind all of the above, in Excel (on the Who owes tab).
    Built from Invoices, lines, payments, allocations, bank lines, opening balances and funding, as stored. Its forecast sheet ties to Income forecast.

Finance (full)

  • DashboardActual
    Outstanding, overdue and the biggest debts — for balances, rely on Who owes. The Projected income tile is an estimate.
    Built from Invoices less credit notes and payments. Projected income = average cash received over the last three months.
  • Salary / room and Funded hoursPreview
    In the search list, but this page has no tab for them — the link lands on Dashboard.
    Built from Nothing on this page.
  • ForecastEstimate
    A cash run-rate. Differs from the Forecast report on purpose.
    Built from Past months: what was invoiced and collected. Future months: the last three months’ average cash, carried forward.
  • Forecast reportEstimate
    EyLog-style income by room and funding stream. Has a “data behind this report” Excel download.
    Built from Today’s bookings × day rates × 52/12, plus council funding and a meal charge (a £15/week placeholder where no food price is set). Counts the council’s money as income.
  • InvoicesSame data
    Same rows as Invoices & Payments → Invoices, 50 a page. Rely on Invoices & Payments.
    Built from invoices, same API.
  • ReconcileSource of truth
    The same unmatched queue, plus statements, matching proposals and TFC.
    Built from Bank lines and the payment-matching tables.
  • PaymentsSame data
    The same payments, on a different screen. Rely on Invoices & Payments.
    Built from payments.
  • FundingPreview
    Funded children for the term. The EYPP column is always £0 and the 2-year-old count looks for a funding type nothing uses.
    Built from child_funding for the term that covers today.
  • WagesEstimate
    Wage bill against income.
    Built from Rota hours × hourly rate, with flat 20% tax, 8% NI and 3% pension.
  • PayrollSource of truth
    Payroll runs and each person’s lines.
    Built from Stored payroll runs (from the Paycheck import).

Finance Reports

  • IncomeActual
    Income invoiced, by service month.
    Built from invoices, drafts and demo/test children left out.
  • Aged debtors and Chase listPreview
    Who to chase and how late. Can differ from Who owes — rely on Who owes.
    Built from Invoice less payments split onto it. Ignores credit notes and payments not split onto an invoice.
  • PaymentsActual
    Money received, by date.
    Built from payments, failed, cancelled and refunded left out.
  • FundingPreview
    Funding gap per child. Some under-2s and 2-year-olds are priced at the 3–4 rate.
    Built from Weekly funded hours × weeks × a council rate.

HMRC & Deadlines

  • CalendarReference
    Tax and filing dates. Files nothing.
    Built from Worked out from the year end.
  • Termly reportSource of truth
    Money by term — reconciled months only.
    Built from Xero, the bank, Ealing funding and invoices, for months someone has marked reconciled. Others show a warning, not figures.

Accounts (this page)

  • Accounts homeActual
    Fee runs, payroll totals, the bank summary.
    Built from Invoices grouped by service month (drafts counted, demo/test children not left out, so it can sit a little above the pack), payroll runs, bank lines.
  • Payroll importSource of truth
    Bring in Paycheck’s files.
    Built from Your Paycheck CSVs → payroll runs.
  • Auto-allocateTool
    Bank receipts matched to invoices. Off unless switched on.
    Built from Bank lines and invoices.
  • Ealing fundingEstimate
    Expected vs received, by age group. Expected is an estimate; received is what was recorded.
    Built from Funded hours × funding rates; recorded receipts.
  • Duplicate bank linesTool
    Lines that came in twice.
    Built from Bank lines from more than one feed.
