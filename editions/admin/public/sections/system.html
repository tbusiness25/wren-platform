🏖️ Away Mode
Away mode When ON, Aria greets callers with "The manager is away until [return date]" and offers cover if the deputy is available. Fires an email OOO webhook to N8N.
Note: Toby will switch this ON himself when he leaves. The N8N flow handles the Google Workspace OOO responder. Toggle OFF to revert everything. the deputy is not available Thursdays — Aria will say she's unavailable on that day automatically.
🔕 Do Not Disturb
Do not disturb When ON, James tells callers Toby is currently unavailable and offers the deputy (deputy manager, baby room) instead — no transfer to Toby's mobile/office, and no Telegram pings (urgent/safeguarding still gets through). Takes effect on the next call.
