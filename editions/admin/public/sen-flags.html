SEN Early-Flagging
Decision support, not diagnosis. These are AI-generated patterns noticed across a child's routine nursery records — observations, first words, framework tracker, attendance. Every suggestion cites the specific evidence behind it. Nothing here is a diagnosis or an automatic action; a SENCO or manager reviews every suggestion and decides whether to accept it onto the SEN register, dismiss it, or just mark it reviewed. Generated locally (on-site Ollama) — child data never leaves the building.

What this tool does: looks for patterns across a child's own observations, first-words log, framework tracker and attendance that might be worth a closer human look, and cites the exact evidence for each one.
What it doesn't do: diagnose, label, or take any action on its own. Every suggestion sits here until a SENCO/manager reviews it.
EAL policy: having a home language other than English, and limited English, is normal bilingual development — the model is instructed not to flag communication_language for that alone. It only flags when evidence suggests difficulty across all the child's languages, and explains its EAL reasoning in each suggestion's evidence.
Human decides: dismissal reasons are recorded and reviewed periodically to check the tool is behaving fairly across languages and backgrounds.
