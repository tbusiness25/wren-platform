🔒 Access Control
Allow full portal access from outside school network When OFF, staff accessing from outside the school Tailscale network can only reach the HR portal — no child or learning data. When ON, full access is allowed remotely.
✓ On Tailscale (100.x.x.x) — always full access ✕ Remote — HR only
How this works: Wren checks the requesting IP address. Connections from the 100.x.x.x Tailscale network always have full access. Connections from any other IP are either allowed (toggle ON) or restricted to HR only (toggle OFF). Recommended: keep OFF for safeguarding — staff should use Tailscale for remote access to child data.

🏛️ Council Data Sharing
Share anonymous totals with the council Off by default. When on, each term Wren sends the council COUNTS ONLY — never a child's name, DOB or any note.
Show our setting's name to the council Off = the borough dashboard counts us but never names us. On = our name appears next to our own figures only.
Exactly what gets sent, each term: headcounts by age band (0-2, 2-3, 3-4, 4+); for each EYFS area, how many children are on track for their age vs not yet, split by age band; the same split for funded 2-year-olds, SEND, EAL and gender; a count of school-leaver transition records shared this term. Any number under 5 is withheld — sent as "too few to show", never as a figure, and a whole on-track/not-yet split is withheld together if either side is small (so the hidden number can't be worked out from the total). No child's name, DOB, note or anything free-text ever leaves this building.

🚪 Room Moves

      

        When a child moves up a room, Wren moves their booking too — occupancy and the
        register count the booking, not the profile, so this is what keeps the room numbers honest.
        Runs once a night as part of the existing nightly job (no separate schedule).

                you move children from Admissions → Transfer window. ON — the job runs each night

                the first move should be one you have agreed in the building.

🌱 Admissions pipeline

      

        

          Apply email-detected stage moves automatically
          When an email from a known family says a viewing is booked, a viewing happened, or
            they'd like a place, Roost can suggest moving their pipeline card forward — a chip on
            the card, Accept/Dismiss. Off by default: suggestions wait for a person to accept them.
            Turning this on applies a clear match straight away instead (never backwards, never an
            automatic email to the family).
