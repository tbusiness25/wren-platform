🧭 Signals

Wren watching the nursery and speaking first — invoices going out in three days that nobody has started, a September with half the roll leaving, an open safeguarding concern with nothing written against it. Each one is a question asked in SQL. Silence is the normal answer, and a signal that finds nothing sends nothing.

Nothing here is a scheduled reminder: a signal fires because of what the data says, and stops the moment the thing is done. Every notice says what it counted, so you can check it against the screen it links to. If one of them turns out to be noise, mute it — and if you dismiss the same one three times running without the finding changing, Wren mutes it for you and tells you it has.

Banners (added 28 Oct): switch "Show as a banner" on and that signal also appears as a strip at the top of every Roost page whenever it has something to say — even while signals are off, and it keeps showing after the due date until the job is done. Invoices are on by default.
