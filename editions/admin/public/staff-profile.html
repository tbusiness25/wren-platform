Staff details

Early Years Census qualification

Feeds the DfE Early Years Census (Roost → Funding → Census). Blank "full & relevant level" falls back to the qualification level on this profile's Payroll/HR record.

Payroll

What this person gave us on their starter form, so we can operate PAYE. Wren does not file anything with HMRC — for RTI use BrightPay, Sage or Xero Payroll.

Publish rights

When off, this person's observations save to the Review Queue (Staff → Review → Observations) instead of publishing straight to parents — a manager, deputy or the room leader approves or returns them. Turn on once you're confident in their judgement.

Area of responsibility

Each practitioner looks after one of the seven EYFS areas: each half-term they say what they have noticed and what they would change, and they choose the next half-term's intentions from the next steps Wren gathers out of the children's observations. An area can be shared, and nobody has to have one.

Google sign-in

Staff can sign in to Roost and Seed with their work Google account instead of a PIN. The account is matched to this record by email address — Google proves who they are, Wren decides what they can do. Their PIN keeps working either way.

Login PIN

The PIN this person types on the tablet and on Roost. Without one they cannot sign in at all — which is what you want for a leaver. Removing a PIN does not touch their record, observations or history.

Leaving

Record that this person has left: their last working day, why, and any note. A last day of today or earlier takes them off the rota, ratios, staff lists and sign-in straight away; a later date takes effect the night after it. Their records are kept, and Reactivate undoes it.

Absence & holiday

Recent absence records and holiday entitlement.

Training & CPD

CPD records and course completions.

Add a training record

A course or certificate for this person — e.g. an Ealing-run safeguarding refresher. Appears in CPD & courses above once saved.

Supervisions

Supervision records for this staff member.

Documents

Certificates, contracts, reports and other files for this staff member. Visibility controls who can see each file.

Strengths & voice

What they wrote about themselves and how we work. Named, and read-only here — only they can change or delete it.
