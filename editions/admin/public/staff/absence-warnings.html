Nothing here reaches a member of staff on its own. When somebody crosses a trigger that is switched on, Wren writes a draft letter and tells you. They are told nothing until you have read that draft and pressed Approve — and approving it opens a disciplinary case at the concern step, never at an outcome. An outcome before an invitation and a meeting is not a record of a fair process, and the disciplinary screen refuses one regardless of what is pressed here.
