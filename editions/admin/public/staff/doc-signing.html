A receipt records the version, not just the document. "Everyone signed the safeguarding policy" means nothing if the policy has been edited since — so a policy receipt pins policy:v4 and a document receipt pins the file's upload timestamp. Re-publish or re-upload and the old receipts visibly refer to the old version instead of quietly appearing to cover the new one. Signing a policy here also writes the usual policy_acknowledgments row, so the HR policies screen stays correct.
