Lateness is measured from one source only — the clock-in events on the door and the sign-in board. A shift with no recorded arrival is not counted as on time; it is not counted at all. A grace period (set in Settings, five minutes by default) is applied before anything is called late, but the minutes shown are counted from the scheduled start, not from the end of grace.
