How the allowance is worked out

Bank holidays

Cached from the gov.uk England-and-Wales list. Every allowance is worked out from this cache, never from the internet, so nobody's balance changes because gov.uk is having a bad day.

Opening balances — the switch from BrightHR

Paste or upload one row per person with the figure from BrightHR. Preview first: nothing is written until you say so, and a row that does not match exactly one member of staff is reported rather than guessed at.
