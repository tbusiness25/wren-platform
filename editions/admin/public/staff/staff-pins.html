Staff PINs
Who can sign in on the PIN pad — set, reset or remove a PIN without opening a profile
← Staff
Wren never shows a PIN. A PIN is stored as a one-way hash, so it cannot be read back — not by this page, not by a manager, not by Wren. It can only be replaced. What you see here is whether someone has one, how many digits it is, and when it was last changed.
Removing a PIN locks that person out of the PIN pad immediately. That is the point of it: deactivating a leaver's record takes them off the rota but does not, on its own, stop them signing in.
