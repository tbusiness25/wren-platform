📅 Term dates

Used for planning: half-terms, weekly plans and the breaks in between.
Six half-terms an academic year, and the holidays between them. This is what the planner counts weeks against, what decides whether this week is plannable, and when staff are prompted to evaluate a half-term and write termly updates. The summer holiday is a break like any other — it belongs in the list below.
These are not the funding periods. Entitlement hours, rates and what the council pays live on Settings → Funding dates →. The two calendars are separate on purpose: funding runs continuously, teaching does not.
