🗓️ Termly Plan

Term → theme → learning intentions → books, songs and events; then six weeks of continuous provision and planned activities per playroom; then the end-of-term evaluation.
How this fits together. The intentions come from your setting's active framework (grouped area → sub-area), the events come from your existing calendar, and the weekly cards come from the same resource-card library and activity bank your staff use when writing a child's next steps — nothing here is a separate copy.
