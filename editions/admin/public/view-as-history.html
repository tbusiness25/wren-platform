👁️ View-as history

Every time a manager opened Finch or Seed as somebody else: who looked, whose account, when, and every page they saw. A view-as session is read only and lasts thirty minutes — nothing on this list could have changed, sent or saved anything.

This list is the reason Wren does not have a master password. With a shared password every login looks like the real person, and afterwards there is no way to answer “did the parent do that, or did we?” — which is exactly the question a safeguarding or GDPR complaint turns on.
