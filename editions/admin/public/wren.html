What Wren is

Wren is the system we run the nursery on — the same one the staff use for the register, the planning and their record-keeping. Parents get their own part of it, so you can see your child's day as it happens rather than hearing about it at pick-up.

It works in any web browser, on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install, though you can add it to your home screen so it behaves like an app.

What you can do in it

Not everything applies to every child — the baby room logs more of the day than the pre-school does, and some of these appear only when they are relevant to you.

📔

Your child's day

Meals and what they actually ate, sleeps, nappies or toileting, and how the day went — logged by the room team as it happens, not written up afterwards.

🌱

Photos and observations

The moments the staff notice and write down: what your child was doing, what it shows about how they are getting on, and what we plan to do next with them.

💬

Messages

Message the room team directly instead of catching someone at the door. Everything is kept in one thread, so nothing gets lost on a scrap of paper.

📚

What we are planning

The half-term plan for your child's room — the theme, the books we will share, the songs we will sing and the activities behind it, so you can pick some of it up at home.

📅

Dates, menus and newsletters

What is on, what is for lunch, and the half-term newsletter — in one place, and always the current version rather than whichever letter is still on the fridge.

✅

Forms, consents and invoices

Sign consents, upload a document, report an absence, and see your invoices and what has been paid, without printing anything.

Getting in

There is no password to remember.

  1. Go to parents.example-nursery.co.uk — or tap the button on this page.
  2. Enter the email address you gave us when your child started.
  3. We email you a short code. Type it in, and you are through.
If the email does not arrive, check your junk folder first, then tell us — it usually means we have a different address on file for you, which takes us a moment to fix. We are also working on a quicker way to sign in; we will write to you separately when it is ready, and nothing you do today will stop working.

Tell us what to change

The app has a feedback button, and it comes straight to the manager.

  1. Tap More at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll to Help & tools.
  3. Choose Tell us what to change for anything you like, dislike or wish it did — or Report a bug if something is broken or will not load.

Where your child's information lives

Wren runs on our own equipment, here at the nursery. Your child's records, photos and messages are not handed to a third-party company to hold for us, and they are not used to train anything.

Only the staff who need to see a child's records can see them, and every look is logged. You can ask us for a copy of everything we hold about your child at any time — there is a button for that in the app, under Settings → My Data.
