✨ AI-assisted planning

Each of us looks after one of the seven areas. Each half-term you say what you have noticed and what you would change — then Wren gathers every child's current next steps in your area, groups them, and hands you the list to tick. You choose intentions from real observations; you do not write them from scratch. Step 4 goes further: Wren reads all your key children's observations, next steps and termly updates and drafts the whole half-term — theme, intentions, activities, resources and a week-by-week focus — for you to edit.
