🪶 Fledge — our progress framework
How we understand and support your child's learning and development.

What is Fledge?

Fledge is how we notice how your child is getting on — it is not what we teach. What we teach is our curriculum planning: the theme for each half-term, the books we read, the songs we sing and the activities behind them, which you can see on the Planning page. Fledge is the picture we hold alongside it of what children can typically do as they grow.

A wren fledges when it's ready to leave the nest. Fledge is the curriculum we use to picture your child's journey — from their very first days with us to the day they're ready for school.

Instead of ticking boxes, we notice where your child is on that journey across seven areas of learning (like talking & listening, making friends, moving & growing, early reading and early maths). We use five gentle stages called bands to describe the path.

The five bands

B1
Nestlings
birth – 18 months
The very beginning — settling in, feeling safe, and discovering the world through senses and play.
B2
First flutters
18 months – 2½ years
Growing in confidence — first words, first friendships, and lots of busy exploring.
B3
Finding wings
2½ – 3½ years
Ideas take off — longer conversations, pretend play, and beginning to do things independently.
B4
Stretching wings
3½ – 4½ years
Getting ready to soar — sharing, problem-solving, early mark-making and a love of stories.
B5
Ready to fledge
4½ years – starting school
Confident and curious — ready for the next big adventure of school.

It's a lens, not a test. Bands describe the journey, not a target or a race. Children move at their own pace, and it's completely normal for your child to be in different bands for different areas. We use everyday moments — play, chat, curiosity — to notice where they are, and what would help them next. There's nothing for your child to pass or fail.
