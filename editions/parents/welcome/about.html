What Cyber Essentials means

Cyber Essentials is the UK government-backed cyber security standard — Your Nursery holds this certification, which means our systems have been independently checked against a recognised set of security controls.

🌱 What Wren is

This is your parent hub — the app built by Your Nursery, for you. It's how we share your child's day: their observations and photos, the daily diary, messages with their key person, the weekly menu, invoices and funding, and all the small everyday moments in between.

It isn't a third-party product bolted onto the nursery — Your Nursery built and runs it ourselves, so it works exactly how our nursery works, and we can keep improving it based on what you tell us.

📱 How to use it — quick start

Sign in with just your email

No password to set up or forget. Enter the email we hold for you, we'll send a one-time code, and you're in — the hub finds your child automatically.

See observations, photos and the diary

Your Learning Journey shows what your child's key person has chosen to share; the Daily Diary covers feeds, naps and nappies through the day.

Message the team

Use Messages to reach your child's key person, the office or the manager directly — attach a photo if it helps.

Report an absence in a few taps

Open Report an Absence, pick sickness or holiday and the dates — it reaches the team straight away and updates the register, no need to ring as well.

Check invoices and funding

Invoices & Fees shows your funding, invoice history and balance — how to pay is shown on each invoice, and the office can always talk you through it.

💬 Quick questions

Is this really secure?

Yes — it runs on our own on-site system behind secure email sign-in, we're Cyber Essentials certified, and any AI features run on our own hardware rather than sending data to outside companies.

Who can see my child's information?

Only you (and anyone else whose email we hold for your child) and the nursery staff caring for them. Nothing is shared with other parents or outside organisations.

Can I ask for my child's data, or ask you to delete it?

Yes, any time — use the My Child's Data tile. A manager reviews every request before anything is released or removed.

I've got another question about the hub.

The office is always happy to help — see the contact details below, or use Messages to ask the team directly.
