Request CCTV Footage 📹

Ask us to look at footage your child appears in. Every request is recorded, reviewed by our manager, and answered.

Please tell us straight away. Footage is kept for 7 days and then automatically overwritten. Once it is gone we cannot get it back — whatever the deadline for answering you says. If you think something may need looking at, send this today.

What happens next
Our manager checks who you are, looks at whether other children or adults appear in the same footage, and decides whether we can share it, share it with other people obscured, or explain why we cannot. We answer within one calendar month — but we act on the footage itself within days, because that is how long it exists.
