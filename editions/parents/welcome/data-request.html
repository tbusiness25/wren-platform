My Child's Data 🔐

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of your child's data, or ask us to delete it. Requests are reviewed by our manager before anything is released or removed.

Make a request

What we hold, and what each request does

📂 Get a copy of the data

We'll gather everything we hold about your child — observations, photos, their learning journey and records — into a copy you can download (a lovely readable memory book plus a machine-readable file). Released once our manager has approved it.

🗑️ Request deletion

We'll remove your child's personal records once approved. Please note: some records we are legally required to keep for a set period (safeguarding, accident/medication records, financial and attendance registers) will be retained and kept secure — everything else is erased. Nothing is ever deleted without manager approval.
