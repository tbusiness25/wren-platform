Why we ask for this

The government asks every family claiming funded early-education hours to complete a short declaration each term. It lets us claim your funded hours from Ealing Council on your behalf.

Section 1 — Your child's details

Your child's name, date of birth and home address come from our records — update them in Our Details if anything's changed.

Section 2 — Parent / Carer 1

Parent/Carer 1 must be the person who applied for the working-parent code.

We need both your date of birth and your National Insurance number: Ealing Council asks for both to pay your funded hours.

Required — Ealing Council needs it to pay your funded hours. If you don't have one, give your Asylum Support reference below instead.

Partner (if applicable)

A partner must live at the same address as your child. Leave blank if not applicable.

Section 3 — Entitlement you're claiming

This is the eligibility code you got from the childcare service (childcarechoices.gov.uk). We reconfirm it with HMRC each term.

Total funded hours across all settings can't exceed your entitlement (15 or 30 hours/week). Tell us if your child is funded anywhere else.

Eligibility checks (opt-in)

Ticking yes lets us run the appropriate checks — your child could receive extra funding (Early Years Pupil Premium or the Disability Access Fund). Optional.

Section 6 — Your declaration

Please read and tick each statement. You must agree to all of them for us to claim your funding.

Section 6 — Sign

By signing I confirm the information is accurate and true, I agree to the conditions, and I authorise Your Nursery to claim free entitlement funding on behalf of my child. Data is shared with the provider and LB Ealing under the Childcare Acts 2006 & 2016 and the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. Typing your name acts as your electronic signature (Electronic Communications Act 2000).
