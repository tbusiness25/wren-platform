Files you upload go straight to your child's key person and the nursery managers. Anything shared with you here has been made visible by the nursery.
What we ask families for

Reports, certificates and forms your child's key person has shared with you — plus any files you upload, such as red-book pages or consent scans.

  • Family photo — we print it at A4 for the room display.
  • Photo for their peg — head and shoulders, printed about 45 × 55 mm.
  • Allergy documentation — a letter from your GP, or an allergy action plan.
  • Medicine or health document — a prescription, care plan or instructions.

We remove the hidden location data phones add to photos before storing anything. The two photos are yours — we offer them back and delete them when your child leaves. Only nursery staff can see anything you upload here.
