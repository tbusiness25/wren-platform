Logging in — just your email

There's no password to set up or forget. Here's how it works the first time:

  1. Open parents.example-nursery.co.uk in your phone or computer's browser.
  2. Type in the email address you gave us when your child joined.
  3. We'll email you a one-time code (a short number). Check your inbox, and pop the code in.
  4. That's it — you're in, and the hub finds your child automatically from your email.

After the first time, your device is remembered, so the hub usually just opens straight away.

If it says it can't find your child: you may have used a different email from the one we hold, or your little one may have only just started (please allow up to 24 hours after enrolment). Give the office a ring on 020 7946 0000 and we'll sort it.
Add it to your phone's home screen

This puts a Your Nursery icon on your phone so the hub opens like an app — full-screen, one tap, no typing the address each time.

On an iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the hub in Safari (this only works in Safari, not Chrome, on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner. The Your Nursery icon appears on your home screen.

On an Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Open the hub in Chrome.
  2. You may see a little "Install the Your Nursery app" banner pop up — just tap Install.
  3. No banner? Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) and choose Add to Home screen (or Install app), then confirm.

Once it's added, open it from that icon and it behaves just like an app. It even shows a friendly screen if you're briefly offline.

Messages and photos

The hub is how we share your child's moments with you, and it's built to keep those private to your family.

  • You'll only ever see what we choose to share. An observation, a diary note or a photo appears in your Learning Journey only when your child's key person has ticked "share with parents". Nothing goes out automatically.
  • Photos are approved first. Any photo a staff member sends is checked and approved by a manager before it reaches you — a simple safeguarding step so the right photo goes to the right family, every time.
  • Getting in touch. The quickest way to tell us about an absence is the Report an Absence tile. For anything else, the phone numbers and email are at the bottom of every page.
Reporting an absence
  1. Open Report an Absence from the home screen.
  2. Choose your child, then Sickness / absence or Holiday.
  3. Pick the first and last day off, and add a quick note if you'd like (e.g. "high temperature").
  4. Tap Submit absence.

It reaches the team straight away and updates the register, so there's no need to ring as well. Your recent reports are listed underneath so you can see they've gone through.

Your privacy

Your child's information stays with the nursery. It lives on our own system, behind your secure email sign-in, and you only ever see your own child — never anyone else's. We don't sell or share your data with advertisers, and where the nursery uses any "smart" features they run on our own computers on-site — nothing about your child is sent to third-party AI services like ChatGPT. Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of your child's data, or ask us to delete it, using the My Child's Data tile; a manager reviews every request before anything is released or removed.

Quick questions

I've got a new phone — how do I get back in?

Open parents.example-nursery.co.uk, enter the same email, pop in the one-time code we send you, and add it to your new home screen. Nothing is lost.

I'm not getting the sign-in code.

Check your spam/junk folder, and that you're using the email address we hold for you. Still stuck? Call the office and we'll check the address on file.

Can both parents/carers have access?

Yes — anyone whose email we hold for your child can sign in the same way. Ask the office to add a second email if needed.

A photo I was expecting hasn't appeared.

Photos are held for a manager to approve before they reach you, so there can be a short wait. If something seems missing, just ask your child's key person.

Can I pay my invoice in the hub?

You can view invoices, funding and your balance here; how to pay is shown on the invoice, and the office can always talk you through it.
