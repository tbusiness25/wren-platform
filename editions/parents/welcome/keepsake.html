About the memory keepsake

When your child leaves us, we make you a keepsake of their whole time at Your Nursery — photos, videos, milestones and their learning journey. It is free, and it is yours to keep.

The book covers everything up to the day you make it. Make it again later and the new one includes anything added since; the old link stops working when you do.
