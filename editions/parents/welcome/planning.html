☀️ Summer at Your Nursery

Over the summer we spend most of our time doing summer activities and games, with less of a focus on structured learning and big group fun physical activities — splashing around, eating ice lollies, making memories with our older children leaving to go to school, and settling our new children who are starting over the summer. You will of course still be getting observations and will still be learning through play!
