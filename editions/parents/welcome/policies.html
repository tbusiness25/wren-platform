Anything marked Needs signing is waiting for you. Everything else is here to read whenever you want it, including our terms and conditions.

Why we ask you to sign

A signature is against one version of a document. If we ever change a document you have signed, your old signature is kept as a record but you will be asked to read and sign the new version — we never carry a signature across, because that would mean claiming you agreed to something you had not read.

Most documents here do not need a signature. Our policies and procedures are updated as we learn and as the law changes, and you agreed to them when you registered — we tell you when something changes, but we do not ask you to sign each update. Our CCTV notice is the same: the cameras are there to keep children safe, which is our own legitimate interest and not something we ask you to consent to. Where a document genuinely is your choice — photographs in particular — it lives under Permissions, where you can change it whenever you like.
We record the date and time, and the device and network address you signed from. That is all — the same details we already keep when you sign anything else here, and we keep them so we can show, if we are ever asked, exactly what was agreed and when.
