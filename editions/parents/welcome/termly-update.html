Your child’s termly update 📖

Every half-term, your child’s key person writes you a short update: how last term went, what we’re working on now, and a few things you could try at home.

What a termly update is

Roughly every six weeks — following Ealing’s school term dates, so it lands at the start of each half-term and the breaks fall where ours do — your child’s key person writes you an update. The whole room gets theirs at the same time.

Each one has five parts:

  • What went well last term — what your child got stuck into, and what they can do now that they couldn’t before.
  • What we’re working on this term — and what that looks like in the room.
  • Next steps — the specific things we’re helping your child with, and the activities and resources we’ve chosen for each one.
  • Things you could try at home — two or three simple ideas using things you already have. Ten minutes each. No homework.
  • Their tracking data — where your child is across the seven areas of learning, and what has moved since last time.

Being honest with you

We write what our records actually show. If we haven’t gathered enough about something yet, the update says so rather than filling the space with something we can’t show you the evidence for. Your key person reads and approves every single update before it reaches you — nothing is sent automatically.

What we would love from you

Tell us what your child has been enjoying at home. Most of our best next steps start with something a parent mentioned at the door.
