Your child’s 2 year check 🩺

A short written summary of how your child is getting on, ready for you to take to your health visitor appointment.

What the 2 year check is

Every nursery in England has to write a short summary for children between their second and third birthdays. It is called the progress check at age two. It is not a test, and it is not a report on everything your child does with us.

What it covers

Just the three things that matter most at this age:

  • Talking and understanding — how your child listens, follows what is going on, and makes themselves understood.
  • Moving and using their hands — walking, climbing, balance, and things like holding a crayon or using a spoon.
  • Feelings, friendships and doing things for themselves — settling, playing with other children, and managing things like coats and hand-washing.

We also add a short bit about how your child likes to learn — what they get absorbed in, whether they stick at things, and the ideas they come up with themselves.

Why it matters to you

Somewhere between two and two and a half, your health visitor will invite you to a review of your child’s development. The check we write is designed to sit alongside that appointment, so the people looking after your child are all working from the same picture. Please take it with you and share it with your health visitor.

We can write it any time after your child turns two, and we aim to have it done by around two and a half — so it is ready before that appointment rather than after it. If your review is coming up sooner, tell us above and we will write it in time.

Being honest with you

We write what our records actually show — including anything your child is not doing yet, or that we are still working on. If there is something we are concerned about, we will say so, and we will write down what we plan to do about it, agreed with you. If we have not yet gathered enough about a particular area, we will say that too, rather than making something up.

How it works here

Tell us above as soon as your health visitor has been in touch — the date if you have one, and without it if you do not. Our Pre-school team then write the summary from our own records of your child, check it over, put their name to it, and send it to you here as a PDF you can print or show on your phone. Your child’s key person can read and add to it too, wherever they work.

If your child is not two yet we make a note of your request and write the check once they turn two — the summary has to describe a two-year-old, so it cannot be written before then.
