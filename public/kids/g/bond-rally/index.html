🎈 Bond Rally

Number bonds and facts to 20, until they are automatic. Free, no login, no adverts, works offline.

Play Bond Rally

What it practises

Number bonds and addition and subtraction facts to 20, practised for recall.

KS1 Mathematics. Year 1: "represent and use number bonds and related subtraction facts within 20". Year 2: "recall and use addition and subtraction facts to 20 fluently, and derive and use related facts up to 100".

Age and stage

Key Stage 1 (Years 1–2) · ages 5–7 · KS1 maths: number bonds

Levels

Five: bonds to 10 with the bar, bonds to 10, bonds to 20, subtraction within 20, missing number in any position. The game moves a child up after three right in a row and down after two wrong, so it settles where they are rather than where a year group says they should be.

How it works

Quick facts with four answers to choose from. Levels 1 and 2 show the bond as a part-whole bar first. Facts the child gets wrong are kept on the device and brought back round sooner.

Why it is built this way

"Fluently" is the word that matters: a child who works out 7 + 6 on their fingers every time has no working memory left for the actual problem. Facts become recall through retrieval practice spaced over time, not through more worked examples. There is deliberately no countdown clock — timing pressure is what makes fluency practice frightening for exactly the children who most need it.

Away from the screen

One minute a day out loud beats twenty minutes once a week. "I say six, you say four." Count out ten and hide some — how many are hidden?

A round takes two to three minutes, and the app prompts a break after about five. For under-fives, UK guidance is clear that what matters most is that screen time is short and shared with an adult who talks to them about it — so sit with them if you can.
