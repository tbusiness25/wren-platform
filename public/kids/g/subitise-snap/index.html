🎲 Snap Count

See how many without counting. Free, no login, no adverts, works offline.

Play Snap Count

What it practises

Subitising — recognising how many without counting.

EYFS statutory framework, area of learning: Mathematics. Early Learning Goals: ELG11b, ELG11a.

Age and stage

EYFS (under 5s) · ages 3–5 · Early maths and number sense

Levels

Five: dice to 3, dice to 5, ten-frame to 6, scatter to 6, two groups to 10. The game moves a child up after three right in a row and down after two wrong, so it settles where they are rather than where a year group says they should be.

How it works

Spots flash on a plate for under a second, then the plate is covered and the child chooses how many. Dice faces first, then ten-frames, then scatters, then two separated groups so six reads as "four and two".

Why it is built this way

Subitising is the strongest early predictor of number fluency, and it is one of the few things a screen practises better than counters, because it needs the dots to appear and then disappear. A child who has time to count has not subitised.

Away from the screen

Roll a dice and say the number before you count. Put three raisins on the table and ask "how many?" without counting. Flash fingers.

A round takes two to three minutes, and the app prompts a break after about five. For under-fives, UK guidance is clear that what matters most is that screen time is short and shared with an adult who talks to them about it — so sit with them if you can.
