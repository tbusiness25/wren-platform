🚂 Ten Town Train

How many more to fill the train? Free, no login, no adverts, works offline.

Play Ten Town Train

What it practises

The composition of numbers to 10 — "how many more to make ten?".

EYFS statutory framework, area of learning: Mathematics. Early Learning Goals: ELG11a, ELG11c.

Age and stage

EYFS (under 5s) · ages 4–6 · Early maths and number sense

Levels

Five: make 5, make 10, make 10 with bigger gaps, make any number to 10, and the inverse. The game moves a child up after three right in a row and down after two wrong, so it settles where they are rather than where a year group says they should be.

How it works

Some carriages are full, some are empty. The child says how many more are needed, and then watches the train fill. Later levels turn it round: the train was filled, how many were full before?

Why it is built this way

The Reception goal is "a deep understanding of numbers to 10, including the composition of each number" — knowing 7 is 5 and 2, and 6 and 1, and 4 and 3. That is different from counting, and it is what becomes number bonds in Year 1. The empty carriages mean the answer is a picture before it is a numeral.

Away from the screen

Five cups out and seven people — how many more? Count the gaps in an egg box. "We have three, how many more for six?"

A round takes two to three minutes, and the app prompts a break after about five. For under-fives, UK guidance is clear that what matters most is that screen time is short and shared with an adult who talks to them about it — so sit with them if you can.
